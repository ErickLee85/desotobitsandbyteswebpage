Why keep AI in-house

Your AI. Your hardware. Your data.

Cloud AI tools are convenient, but every prompt you type and every file you upload is processed on someone else's servers under someone else's data policy. On-premise AI gives your team the same productivity boost while you stay in full control.

Data never leaves

Contracts, patient notes, financials, and customer records stay on your network. Nothing is sent to OpenAI, Google, or anyone else.

No per-seat AI bills

Open-source models have no monthly subscription or per-token charges. Add users without watching a usage meter climb.

Compliance-friendly

Keeping data on-site makes it far easier to meet HIPAA, legal privilege, and client confidentiality requirements than sending it to a public AI service.

Works offline

Your AI keeps answering even if the internet goes down or a cloud vendor has an outage, changes pricing, or retires a model.

Chat with your files

Connect the AI to your NAS or file server and ask questions about your own documents, SOPs, and archives in plain English.

Built for your team

A clean, browser-based chat interface with user accounts, so anyone in your office can use it on day one with no command line required.

Security first

Private by design, verified in practice

Running AI locally is the foundation, but we don't stop there. Every deployment is locked down so only your people can reach it, and so you can confirm nothing is quietly leaving your network.

  • VPN with multi-factor authentication Remote and mobile access goes through your own VPN with MFA. The AI server is never exposed directly to the internet.
  • Container isolation Models and the chat interface run in isolated Docker containers with only the access they need.
  • Outbound traffic controls & monitoring Firewall rules block unnecessary outbound connections, and we can monitor network traffic so you can verify no data leaves.
  • Open-source transparency We use widely reviewed open-source software and models, and keep them patched and up to date.
  • User accounts & access control Each person gets their own login, and admins control who can use which models and document collections.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is on-premise AI?

On-premise AI is a ChatGPT-style assistant that runs entirely on hardware you own, inside your office. Prompts, answers, and documents are processed locally and are never sent to a third-party cloud provider. It's a great fit for law firms, medical and dental offices, accountants, and any DeSoto County business that handles sensitive information.

Do I need expensive hardware to run AI locally?

Not necessarily. Smaller 7–8 billion parameter models run on a modern workstation with 16–32 GB of RAM, even without a GPU. A dedicated GPU and more memory make responses faster and let you support more users and larger models. We'll recommend the right setup during your free assessment.

Can my team use the private AI outside the office?

Yes. We set up a VPN with multi-factor authentication so approved staff can securely reach your AI from laptops and phones, without exposing the server to the public internet.

How good are local models compared to ChatGPT?

Modern open-source models like Llama and IBM Granite handle drafting, summarizing, and answering questions about your documents very well. The largest cloud models are still stronger at some complex reasoning tasks, so we'll help you decide what belongs on-premise and where a cloud AI integration might make sense.

Are there monthly fees?

The open-source models and software we deploy have no per-user or per-token fees. Your costs are the hardware, our setup, and an optional support plan for updates, new models, and monitoring.
