My Story

From the Army to architecting software.

I served as a soldier in the United States Army and deployed twice to Iraq during the war. The military taught me how to operate under pressure, lead small teams, and ship the mission no matter how rough the conditions. Those lessons are the foundation of how I run Desoto Bits & Bytes today.

After transitioning out of service, I picked up code and never put it down. Over the last five years I've shipped real software for real customers — from polished mobile apps and complex web applications to fast company websites, custom API integrations, and AI-powered automations.

Today I'm based in Olive Branch, MS, partnering with businesses across DeSoto County, Memphis, and the wider Mid-South. Every project gets the same thing: clear communication, honest pricing, and software built to last.
